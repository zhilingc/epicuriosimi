      how to play

      every day there is one secret ingredient — always something common. type a guess and you get three scores out of 100:

      • pairs well with — how often the two cook together in real recipes
      • shares flavor profile — how many flavor compounds they share
      • similar to — how alike they are, by what they are and how they cook

      each score also shows its heat: cold tepid warm hot. hot means your guess is among the ten closest ingredients on that measure.

      find the ingredient to win. a new one arrives each day.
